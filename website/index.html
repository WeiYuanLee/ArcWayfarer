GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Which devices and operating systems are supported?

ArcWayfarer supports iPhones connected by USB on macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows 10 / 11.

Does my iPhone need to be jailbroken?

No. Connect your iPhone by USB, unlock it, and trust the computer when prompted.

What happens the first time I set a location?

ArcWayfarer may download and mount the Apple developer service image required for your device. This can take a few moments; future use is faster.
